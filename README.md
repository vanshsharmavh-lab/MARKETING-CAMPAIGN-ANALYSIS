# Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes digital marketing campaign data to understand campaign performance, customer engagement, revenue generation, and profitability.

The goal is to identify which platforms, campaign objectives, and marketing strategies provide better business results.

## Objectives

- Analyze marketing campaign performance
- Measure CTR, conversion rate, CPC, and ROAS
- Compare platform performance
- Identify profitable campaigns
- Generate business insights from data

## Dataset Features

The dataset contains information about:

- Campaign details
- Platform and ad placement
- Target audience
- Creative details
- Impressions and clicks
- Conversions
- Ad spend
- Revenue
- Profit
- Marketing KPIs

## Business Insights

Based on the analysis, the following insights were identified:

- High-revenue platforms can be prioritized for future marketing campaigns to improve ROI.
- Campaign objectives with higher profit contribution should receive more budget allocation.
- A higher CTR does not always guarantee higher conversions, so conversion quality should also be monitored.
- ROAS analysis helps identify campaigns that generate better returns compared to their advertising spend.
- Low-performing campaigns can be optimized by improving ad creatives, targeting, and audience selection.
- Customer targeting factors such as age group, income bracket, and purchase intent can help improve campaign effectiveness.
- Marketing teams can reduce unnecessary spending by focusing on campaigns with strong conversion and profitability metrics.

## Recommendations

- Increase budget for high ROAS campaigns.
- Test different creatives and audience segments to improve engagement.
- Continuously monitor CTR, conversion rate, and profit before scaling campaigns.
- Use data-driven decisions instead of only focusing on impressions or clicks.
